# Questions for institutional and clinical review

23 November 2026

For faith institutions: How can women participate in decisions about safeguarding? Where can a person request independent review when a local leader is implicated?

For clinical reviewers: How should a conversational system distinguish a person's statement, the system's inference and a later clarification? What should a concise clinical handoff preserve about timing and uncertainty?

These questions invite methodological review. They contain no patient records. Publication is independent of email dispatch, receipt or endorsement.
