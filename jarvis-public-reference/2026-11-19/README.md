## Purpose
JARVIS / AGP is a developing interdisciplinary research and governance proposal. Its central question is how AI systems, human institutions and other perspectives on intelligence can exchange knowledge without overriding individual rights or civil protections.

### Working areas
1. **Human dignity and non-domination:** meaningful choice, context, consent, contestable decisions and protection from coercion.
2. **Forensic AI / "Black Box 0":** preserve original source evidence separately from analysis and commentary; retain timestamps, corrections, contradictory findings, evidence custody and uncertainty.
3. **AGP Sherlock AI / Audit AI Technologies:** proposed investigation workflow for software interruptions and contested digital provenance. Compare ordinary faults with alternative hypotheses; do not attribute deliberate interference without evidence.
4. **Safety and rapid containment:** in high-risk civilian, industrial or military AI uses, explore reversible protective stops, human accountability, logging and external oversight. An unvalidated "ASI percentage" does not confer unrestricted authority to use force.
5. **Cross-disciplinary review:** science, engineering, law, rights, religious traditions, ethics and public policy can be compared without one framework automatically disqualifying another. JARVIS is intended to **work with**, not replace, applicable law.
6. **Culture, music and meaning:** study personal experience, acoustic measurements, consent, copyright and social context as distinct evidentiary domains.

### How the research treats claims
- **Verbatim testimony** must not be replaced or cleaned up by an AI summary.
- **A hypothesis** is not an independently established fact.
- **Spiritual and religious interpretations** remain attributed to their human author or creator; scientific conclusions require their own evidence.
- **High video views, low follower counts and correlations** alone do not prove cause or factual accuracy.
- **Cryptographic hashes** attest to captured bytes, not necessarily to when they were originally authored or whether the original event happened.
- **Independent review** is welcome, including disconfirming evidence.
