## Research question
How can an institution or AI agent respond **to the actual intent of an inquiry** rather than to an incorrectly assigned category? What controls prevent a generic response or an apparent AI incident from displacing an individual's original testimony, legitimate rights or public-safety requirements?

An institution's form response may be correctly produced within its workflow and still fail to answer the question asked. A language model may time out without providing evidence of who or what caused it. **Neither a template nor a timeout proves a bot, censorship, criminal interference or malice.**

## Mathematical evaluation proposal
Define an inquiry `q_i`, its original expressed intent `I_i`, recipient's lawful remit `J_i`, reply `r_i`, requested components `C_i` and evidence provenance `E_i`.

1. **Relevance/fidelity:** `R_i = sum_j(w_j a_ij)/sum_j(w_j)`, where `a_ij in {0,1}` says whether reply `r_i` actually addresses item `j` and `w_j > 0` are pre-registered weights. Independent reviewers must agree on coding rules and report disagreement. The metric is *not* a substitute for legal interpretation.
2. **Template identity:** `T_ik = 1{ norm(body_i) == norm(body_k) }`. Decide *before comparison* what `norm` removes (e.g., quoted original message versus substantive response). `T=1` is **not** evidence that a bot wrote either message.
3. **Provenance:** store `(source_id, timestamp_source, observed_at, channel, integrity_hash, confidence, privacy_tier, edit_history_status)`; do not mix user-reported and provider times.
4. **Attribution rate:** `p_h = N_human_cause / N_adjudicated`, but undefined when `N_adjudicated = 0`. The phrase “99% human error” is a **hypothesis**, not a validated estimate without incidents, classification procedure, adjudicators and uncertainty intervals.
5. **Equity:** pre-register and evaluate harm and response-relevance rates across legally collected, consented population groups. High audience reach and low income do not by themselves establish that any institution targets a particular class.
6. **Cross-scale pattern testing:** repeated misclassification at user, organizational and regulatory scales may inspire a fractal analogy, but a mathematical fractal needs independently defined scale transforms, invariance tests and baseline comparisons.

### Lexicographic rights floor
The author's JARVIS P-03 proposal treats dignity, proportionality, consent where applicable, lawful process, explanation and meaningful challenge as non-compensable constraints. Candidate designs that violate this floor are **not redeemed by an aggregate score**. This principle is a research proposal for implementation and peer review, not a superseding legal system.

## High-stakes and military-AI safeguards
A proposed “Black Box 0 → 1%” denotes rapid *defensive* escalation to preserve evidence, activate a fail-safe, suspend dangerous automated functions and notify accountable humans. “1%” is **not** an objectively measured percentage of artificial superintelligence. This document does not authorize cyber retaliation, offensive military action, autonomous lethal targeting, essential civilian-service denial or bypassing existing law. International humanitarian law and meaningful human control continue to matter in life-or-death decisions.

## Spiritual/canonical and scientific interfaces
The project's original spiritual themes, including Metatron-inspired field boundaries and religious or celestial imagery, are **human-attributed contributions to ethics and meaning**. They are not removed from original evidence. Here, “fields” can be used as a conceptual design for consent scopes, access boundaries and emergency controls, subject to testing against ordinary safety architecture. No reader is required to accept a supernatural claim to evaluate a technical result, and no worldview warrants discriminatory dismissal.

## Media and public-interest scrutiny
Media and AI systems should label direct observation, quotations, interpretation, satire, unverified hypotheses and corrections distinctly. When considering influential channels, investigate verifiable effects, reach, affected populations, error correction and counter-evidence. **Review should not silently replace an original record.** Criticism is not censorship; no journalist or institution is presumed guilty because it declines to engage with unsolicited research.
