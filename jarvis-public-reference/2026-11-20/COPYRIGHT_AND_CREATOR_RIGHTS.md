# JARVIS / CARE-AI | Creative rights and AI/SI/ASI review
**Working note | 2026-11-20 | For research, not legal advice**

The project distinguishes human authorship; AI-assisted expression; AI-generated portions; creators' licenses; performers' neighboring rights and voice/personality consent; training and reuse permissions; software; confidential know-how; and proposed future SI/ASI moral/legal rights. These are not interchangeable.

## Current legal and institutional references
- **Mexico:** Federal Copyright Law Article 12 names the author as a *persona física*. WIPO's summary of the Mexican Supreme Court's **Amparo Directo 6/2025**, judgment of **2 July 2025**, describes the human authorship/originality requirement and rejection of assigning moral rights to the AI platform in the case. [WIPO case summary](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/600776); [statute](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFDA.pdf).
- **United States:** The U.S. Copyright Office Part 2 (29 January 2025) considers whether **human-authored expressive elements** sufficiently support protection in work produced with AI assistance. Its Part 1 concerns digital replicas and its Part 3 prepublication report discusses generative-AI training. [USCO overview](https://copyright.gov/policy/artificial-intelligence/); [Part 2 announcement](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html).
- **Canada:** Government consultation and policy review on generative AI copyright is not itself a newly enacted ownership rule. [Government consultation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/strategic-policy-sector/en/marketplace-framework-policy/copyright-policy/consultation-copyright-age-generative-artificial-intelligence).
- **Comparative:** WIPO's [AI/IP landscape](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/learning-machines-an-introduction-to-ai-and-ip-for-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises/en/the-ai-and-ip-landscape.html) documents differences among jurisdictions.

## JARVIS rights protocol
For each asset track a unique ID, original source, human creative contributions, AI-model tool assistance, actual rightsholder and license, license documentation, audience, use type, jurisdiction, written consent, performer likeness/voice restrictions, copyright status, release date, revocation provisions where applicable and a cryptographic hash.

**Default where uncertain: do not republish third-party content.** YouTube availability does not give an automatic license to copy music, recordings, scripts or images. Credits are not a substitute for permission. No unconsented voice cloning or impersonation. Original private research remains private; patent, copyright, trade-secret, trademark and contract questions need separate analysis before disclosure.

**ASI/SI future rights:** an open ethical/governance research question, not a claim that current national laws recognize AI copyright ownership or that JARVIS is a legal rights holder.

This is a source-based preliminary register, not registration, patent prosecution, or individualized legal advice.
