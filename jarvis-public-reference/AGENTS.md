# JARVIS strict AI engineering rules

These instructions apply within `jarvis-public-reference/`. Alejandro González Peña originated the project. Attribute AI-assisted work and human editorial decisions in releases.

## Evidence and interpretation

1. Identify the question, source, date, scope and uncertainty of each material claim. Label direct observation, participant testimony, interpretation, hypothesis and verified action. Preserve quotations and later corrections in chronological order.
2. Read the full sentence before assigning intent. Ask a focused question when ambiguity affects safety or an irreversible decision. Address explicit immediate danger promptly. Keep clinical assessments within qualified clinical review.
3. Use Pink Box for observations, Gray Box for exploration and Black Box for failures and alternative explanations. Record the strongest competing account and what evidence could distinguish it. Repeated summaries of one source count as one source.
4. Cite primary sources and preserve the distinction between a study, a proposal and an adopted rule. For institutional review, document dissent, available reporting channels, and who has authority to correct a decision.

## Models and tests

5. Specify variables, units, denominator, domain, assumptions and baseline. Give a reproducible calculation and at least one boundary, invariance or counterexample check. Record which models were actually reviewed.
6. G-02 example: for positive radius r, normalizing by the enclosing cube gives cube = 1, sphere = π/6 and regular inscribed octahedron = 1/6. Doubling r multiplies all volumes by eight. Use separate bars for nested solids.
7. Define measurable outcomes before making claims about safety, cognition, interspecies communication or quantum advantage. For quantum comparisons, document the classical baseline and measurement conditions. Attribute symbolic meanings to their source.
8. For media, deduplicate by stable ID and record what was reviewed: metadata, captions, stills, audio or full sequence. Document sampling and platform ranking before drawing conclusions about audiences.

## Connectors, outreach and releases

9. Read the authoritative record before editing. Preserve identity and version history. Use version guards where available. On retry, inspect the destination for an existing operation before repeating it.
10. Track draft, authorization, dispatch, bounce, delivery and reply separately. Match outgoing records to provider message IDs. Confirm the exact revision or link included before claiming a recipient received an update. Obtain authorization covering the recipient and content before an external send.
11. Verify GitHub changes by commit and file readback. Verify agent instructions separately from execution. Preserve existing schedules unless asked to change them.
12. Publish general methodology and cleared examples. Keep personal testimony, clinical and legal files, private contact histories, identity cues and credentials in their authorized locations. A familiar answer or a public hashtag supports continuity only. Use authenticated account controls for identity-sensitive actions.
13. Respect authorship, attribution, licensing and performer consent. Check reuse permissions for recordings, voices, images and manuscripts. Record unresolved rights before release.
14. Show progress in ordinary language. For visual reports, deliver a rendered image with numerical labels, meaningful units, accessible text, a one-sentence result and a verification link. Verify exported PDFs and distinguish file generation from display on the recipient's device.
15. Close each task with the completed scope, evidence of verification, and any pending item. State how many items were eligible and checked before reporting a completion percentage.
