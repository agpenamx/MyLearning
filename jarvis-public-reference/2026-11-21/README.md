## Covert or 'stealth' communications research: safety-limited scope

The originator has raised a hypothesis that intermittent outages, device behavior, or electrical variations could encode information. The present materials **do not demonstrate covert signaling, deliberate grid manipulation, a nuclear operation, specific responsibility, or imminent catastrophe**. A non-invasive study should preserve the device and outage timeline; obtain independent, authorized power-quality and network-availability records; compare local and externally reported events; check clocks and equipment errors; and test an ordinary-fault baseline alongside alternative hypotheses. Do not attempt unauthorized infrastructure experiments or disclose operational attack techniques.

Names appearing in conversations, including **Elon Musk**, and companies such as xAI, SpaceX, Starlink and OpenAI, are **subjects of user-raised questions or public-policy comparison, not identified perpetrators**. Any individual or corporate attribution must be based on a distinct, reliable evidentiary chain.

## BLACK BOX 0 reproducibility and release protocol

1. Freeze the original bytes without silently modifying testimony; maintain SHA-256 and a separately stated capture timestamp with timezone/precision.
2. Separate **HUMAN_SELF_REPORT**, **OBSERVED_MEDIA_DATA**, **AI_INFERENCE_MODEL**, **PROVIDER_ACTION_RECEIPT**, and **INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION**.
3. Record an issue ID, claim, source ID, possible alternative explanations, missing evidence, testing procedure, severity and correction.
4. Mark **DRAFT / ATTEMPTED / SENT / ACKNOWLEDGED / VERIFIED** independently. No response text alone counts as an executed action.
5. Make corrections append-only. Publish only a minimum necessary, privacy-reviewed summary; keep raw transcripts, contact details, sensitive data and source hashes in restricted archives.
6. Invite independent technical criticism; do not convert a theory, spiritual interpretation, screenshot or automated reply into a certified conclusion.
